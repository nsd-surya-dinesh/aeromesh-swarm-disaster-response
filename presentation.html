OPERATIONAL CONTEXT

Problem Formulation & Post-Disaster Challenges

Post-Disaster Constraints
  • Severed Communications: Earthquake/landslide eliminates terrestrial cellular backhaul; GCS positioned outside hazard zone.
  • BVLOS Terrain Blockage: Dispersed PoIs in rugged 2km x 2km zone with deep mountain ridges causing severe RF Non-Line-of-Sight (NLoS).
  • Battery Limitations: Multicopters have strictly bounded flight endurance; hover at relay points drains 18% more energy than cruising.
  • Dynamic Emergencies: Sudden survivor discoveries, RF jamming, or hardware failure demand instant swarm re-coordination.
AeroMesh-Swarm Innovations
  • Decentralized CBBA Auctioning: Conflict-free task allocation balancing PoI urgency, detour distance, and battery State-of-Charge.
  • Steiner-Point Relay Deployment: Dynamic placement of relay UAVs at geometric Steiner midpoints governed by Artificial Potential Fields (APF).
  • ETX Multi-Hop Mesh Routing: Dynamic Dijkstra routing minimizing Expected Transmission Count (ETX) and packet delivery latency.
  • Sub-2.1s Autonomous Self-Healing: Heartbeat fail-safes detect node failures and instantly promote standby UAVs to restore severed links.
PHYSICAL FOUNDATIONS

Mathematical System Modeling

1. ITU-R P.1411 RF Model
Elevation angle θ determines probabilistic Line-of-Sight:
P(LoS) = 1 / [1 + a*exp(-b(θ-a))]
Path loss with log-normal shadow fading:
PL(d) = 20log10(4πfc*d/c) + η + X_σ
SNR threshold for relay placement:
SNR >= 10 dB
2. Shannon Capacity & ETX
Theoretical link bandwidth modeled dynamically:
C_ij = B * log2(1 + 10^(SNR/10))
Link routing cost via Expected Transmission Count:
ETX_ij = 1 / (d_f * d_r) ~= 1/PDR
Dynamic Dijkstra computes minimum-ETX multi-hop routes.
3. Rotorcraft Aerodynamics
Power combines hover, profile, and parasite drag:
P(t) = P_hov*[c1+c2(v/v0)^2+c3(v/v0)^3] + P_sens + P_rf
Dynamic Return-to-Base (RTB) energy boundary:
E_safe = [P_cr*(||p-p_gcs||/v)/3600] + E_res

SWARM INTELLIGENCE

CBBA Task Allocation & Autonomous Self-Healing

1. CBBA Marginal Utility Scoring
Decentralized bundle bidding without a single master:
S_ij = w_j * γ^(T_arr/60) - α*(Δd/1000) - β*(ΔE/E_cap)
  • Conflict-Free Convergence: Max-bid consensus over the mesh.
  • Battery-Constrained: Rejects tasks exceeding safe RTB reserve.
  • Emergency Preemption: Survivor discoveries (Priority 8.0) immediately preempt lower-priority survey paths.
2. Sub-2.1s Autonomous Self-Healing
Automated replacement scoring for relay failures:
Score(u) = 0.4/||p_u - p_rel|| + 0.3*SoC + 0.3*I(STANDBY)
  • Heartbeat Monitoring: Detects node crash in < 3.0s.
  • Candidate Promotion: Dispatches best standby/scout UAV to relay point.
  • Zero Packet Drop: ETX routing updates routes seamlessly (100% PDR preserved).
